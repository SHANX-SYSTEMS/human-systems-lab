# v0.1.0 — Open-source foundation

SHANX Human Systems Lab v0.1.0 establishes the first public foundation for low-friction, reproducible browser-based human-behavior experiments.

The participant experience is designed around:

**open → play → auto-save → verify receipt → analyze**

## Highlights

- deterministic trial generation from recorded seeds
- local browser persistence for in-progress and unsynced sessions
- generic sync-queue primitives
- public session-envelope schema
- reproducibility/invariant tests
- local-only protocol demo
- research-integrity and security policies
- sanitized ATTENTION-001 v0.5 protocol example

## Research stance

This release does not claim to prove a universal mechanism of attention or human behavior. ATTENTION-001 is included as an example of protocol evolution, falsifiability, and reproducibility.

A failed hypothesis remains a failed hypothesis. Raw observations remain separate from derived analysis.

## Privacy boundary

The public repository contains no private SHANX owner sessions, participant rows, production credentials, private backend identifiers, internal Notion references, or unpublished confidential research outputs.
